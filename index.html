Reconnect HQ

Work through your LinkedIn network: rate each relationship, set a status, snooze, and take notes. Everything stays in this browser.

Bring in your LinkedIn connections

Drop LinkedIn's data export here — the whole .zip, or Connections.csv plus messages.csv. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Drop your export here or
.zip, Connections.csv, messages.csv
  1. On LinkedIn, open Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Choose Connections and Messages (or the full archive) and request it. LinkedIn emails a download link, usually within minutes.
  3. Drop the downloaded file above. Re-import a newer export any time — your ratings, statuses, and notes are kept, and job changes get flagged.

Privacy: this page has no server. Your connections and notes are saved only in this browser's local storage. Use Back up to move them to another browser.

Reconnect HQ — open source, runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking. Stars reflect relationship strength: ★ name recognition, ★★ real exchange, ★★★ would pick up the phone. Priority sorting uses LinkedIn message history only, so relationships you keep up elsewhere won't rank by it.

Settings

Message templates

Placeholders: {first} {name} {company} {position}. Edits to a single card's draft are kept for that person.

Tags

Tags appear as filters and on cards. Add keywords to auto-tag by job title (comma-separated); you can always add or remove a tag on any card.

Keyboard shortcuts

Work through the list without the mouse. Shortcuts are off while you're typing in a field.
